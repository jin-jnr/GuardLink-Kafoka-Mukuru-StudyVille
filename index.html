Welcome to GuardLink

GuardLink is a web-based visitor management system designed for off-campus student hostels. We replace unreliable paper logbooks with a fast, secure, and searchable digital record.

Our Core Features

  • Digital Invitations: Residents send invites directly to guests.
  • Self-Registration: Visitors register online before arrival.
  • Automated Checks: Guards verify entry via QR passes and codes without expensive biometrics.

Notices

  • Offline cache is active at the gate to ensure verification continues during internet outages.
  • No unannounced visitors will be admitted.
