The Problem at Kafoca Mukuru Study Ville

Currently, off-campus student hostels rely on manual paper records and word-of-mouth confirmation. This results in a slow entry process, disputes at the gate, and an inability for management to track who is on the property in real-time.

Our Solution

GuardLink eliminates the gap between manual gatekeeping and proper authorization. Using a software-only approach, residents pre-approve guests, preventing unauthorized access while keeping hardware costs low.
